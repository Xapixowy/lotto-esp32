# Lotto Display

Terminology for showing lottery results on a personal display.

## Language

**Game**:
A named lottery whose draw results appear on the display.

**Draw**:
A scheduled occurrence of a game with its own draw time and winning results.

**Slide**:
The screen showing one game's results.

**Result group**:
A labeled collection of winning values within a game's slide, usually representing a draw.

**Page**:
One screenful of a slide's content when its result groups cannot fit together.

**Screen lock**:
A temporary hold that stops automatic page changes while allowing manual navigation.
_Avoid_: Authentication lock

**Lotto time**:
The time of the last successful retrieval of results from the official Lotto service for the displayed game.

**Sync time**:
The time of the display's last successful retrieval of results from the backend.
